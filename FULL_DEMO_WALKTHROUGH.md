# 🚀 Task Tracker Zeecorp: Ultimate Client Demo Guide

**Target Audience:** Potential Clients, Stakeholders, Executives  
**Goal:** Showcase Zeecorp as an all-in-one Project, Team, and Financial management solution.  
**Theme:** "Efficiency, Transparency, and Collaboration"

---

## 📋 1. Demo Preparation (Pre-Meeting)
*Ensure you have the following ready before sharing your screen:*
*   **Browser**: Clean window with no clutter.
*   **Tabs Open**: 
    1.  Login Page (Start here)
    2.  (Optional) Mailtrap/Email inbox to show notifications
*   **Test Accounts**:
    *   **Admin**: `admin@zeecorp.com` (for high-level overview & setup)
    *   **Manager**: `manager@zeecorp.com` (for project execution)
    *   **Staff**: `staff@zeecorp.com` (for individual contribution)

## 🎬 Part 1: Introduction & Security (2 Minutes)

**Narrative:** *"Task Tracker Zeecorp isn't just a to-do list; it's a secure, role-based ecosystem for your entire organization."*

### 🔹 Action: Login Screen
1.  **Showcase**: Clean, branded login interface.
2.  **Functionality**: 
    *   Secure Authentication (JWT based).
    *   **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Explain that the interface creates custom experiences for Admins, Managers, and Employees.
3.  **Step**: Log in as **Admin**.

---

## 📊 Part 2: The Command Center (Admin Dashboard) (5 Minutes)

**Narrative:** *"As an executive, you need the bird's-eye view. This is your Command Center."*

### 🔹 Action: Admin Dashboard Overview
1.  **KPI Cards**:
    *   **Total Projects**: Active vs Completed.
    *   **Total Revenue**: Point out the **INR (₹)** formatting (e.g., ₹1,200,000) showing real-time financial tracking.
    *   **Workforce Stats**: Total Users, Department distribution.
2.  **Charts & Graphs**:
    *   Show the visual breakdown of project status and revenue trends.

### 🔹 Action: User Management
1.  Navigate to **Users/Admin** section.
2.  **Create New User**:
    *   Demonstrate the form flexibility.
    *   **Highlight**: "Department is optional" – allows for flexible onboarding of consultants or floating resources who don't fit a strict hierarchy.
3.  **Attendance Override**: Briefly show the ability to view/manage staff attendance logs for compliance.

---

## 🚀 Part 3: Project Lifecycle & Financials (8 Minutes)

**Narrative:** *"Now, let's look at how we actually deliver work and track the money."*

### 🔹 Action: Project Management
1.  Navigate to **Projects**.
2.  **Create Project**:
    *   Enter **Budget** & **Revenue**: Show how the system tracks profitability margins immediately.
    *   **Assign Manager**: Delegate responsibility.
3.  **Project Detail View**:
    *   Click into a high-value project (e.g., "AI Implementation").
    *   **Tabs Overview**: Show the clean organization: *Overview, Tasks, Team, Files, Financials*.
    *   **Financial Health**: Show the "Budget vs Spend" progress bars.

---

## 💬 Part 4: Advanced Collaboration (The "Wow" Factor) (8 Minutes)

**Narrative:** *"Communication is where projects die. We solved this with in-context collaboration."*

### 🔹 Action: The Collaboration Hub
1.  Navigate to the **Team / Support / Discussion** tab of a project.
2.  **Threaded Discussions**:
    *   Show a main topic (e.g., "UI Design Review").
    *   Show nested replies keeping the conversation organized, unlike a chaotic chat stream.
3.  **@Mentions (Live Demo)**:
    *   Type a message: *"@staff_user please upload the latest mockups."*
    *   Explain: "This triggers an instant notification and personalized email to that specific user."
4.  **File Attachments**:
    *   Demonstrate uploading a file (Image/PDF) directly to the thread.
    *   Show the **Cloudinary** integration – fast, secure media hosting.

---

## ⚡ Part 5: The Execution Layer (Staff View) (5 Minutes)

**Narrative:** *"How does this look for the team on the ground? Minimal friction, maximum focus."*

### 🔹 Action: Switch to Staff Account
*Log out Admin -> Log in as Staff.*

1.  **Staff Dashboard**:
    *   **"My Tasks"**: A personalized prioritized list. No noise.
2.  **Task Management**:
    *   Open a Task.
    *   Change Status (To Do -> In Progress).
    *   **Add Activity**: Log a comment or attach a file.
3.  **Time Tracking & Attendance**:
    *   **Clock In/Out**: One-click daily attendance.
    *   **Timesheets**: Logging hours against specific projects for accurate billing.
